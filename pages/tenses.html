Simple Present Tense

Kebiasaan, fakta, dan kejadian berulang di masa sekarang

📐 Rumus

KalimatRumus
➕ PositifS + V1 (s/es) + O/C
➖ NegatifS + do/does + not + V1 + O/C
❓ TanyaDo/Does + S + V1 + O/C ?
⚠️ Catatan: Pakai does/does not untuk He, She, It. Pakai do/do not untuk I, You, We, They. Tambahkan -s / -es pada V1 untuk He/She/It di kalimat positif.

⏱️ Kata Penanda (Signal Words)

alwaysusuallyoften sometimesseldomnever every day/weekon Mondays

💬 Contoh Kalimat

➕ She reads a book every morning.
➖ She does not read a book every morning.
❓ Does she read a book every morning?
➕ They go to school by bus.
➖ They do not go to school by bus.

🎯 Kapan Dipakai?

  • Kebiasaan atau rutinitas: "I brush my teeth twice a day."
  • Fakta umum: "The sun rises in the east."
  • Jadwal yang sudah pasti: "The train leaves at 7 AM."

Present Continuous Tense

Kejadian yang sedang berlangsung saat ini

📐 Rumus

KalimatRumus
➕ PositifS + am/is/are + V-ing
➖ NegatifS + am/is/are + not + V-ing
❓ TanyaAm/Is/Are + S + V-ing ?
⚠️ I → am | He/She/It → is | You/We/They → are

⏱️ Kata Penanda

nowright nowat the moment currentlyat this timelook!

💬 Contoh Kalimat

➕ I am studying English right now.
➖ She is not sleeping at the moment.
❓ Are they playing football now?

Present Perfect Tense

Kejadian di masa lalu yang hasilnya masih terasa sekarang

📐 Rumus

KalimatRumus
➕ PositifS + have/has + V3
➖ NegatifS + have/has + not + V3
❓ TanyaHave/Has + S + V3 ?
⚠️ have untuk I/You/We/They | has untuk He/She/It

⏱️ Kata Penanda

💬 Contoh Kalimat

❓ Have you ever been to Bali?

Simple Past Tense

Kejadian yang sudah selesai di masa lampau

📐 Rumus

KalimatRumus
➕ PositifS + V2 + O/C
➖ NegatifS + did + not + V1 + O/C
❓ TanyaDid + S + V1 + O/C ?
⚠️ V2 adalah bentuk kedua kata kerja. Contoh: go → went, eat → ate, study → studied

⏱️ Kata Penanda

yesterdaylast night/week/year agoin 2020just now

💬 Contoh Kalimat

➕ She went to the market yesterday.
➖ I did not watch TV last night.
❓ Did you eat breakfast this morning?

Simple Future Tense

Rencana atau prediksi tentang masa depan

📐 Rumus

KalimatRumus
➕ PositifS + will + V1
➖ NegatifS + will + not + V1
❓ TanyaWill + S + V1 ?
ℹ️ Bisa juga pakai: S + be going to + V1 untuk rencana yang sudah dipikirkan.

⏱️ Kata Penanda

tomorrownext week/monthsoon in the futurelater

💬 Contoh Kalimat

➕ I will visit my grandmother tomorrow.
➖ She will not come to the party.
❓ Will you help me with this?

13

Past Future Tense

Rencana di masa lalu yang sering tidak terlaksana (kalimat pengandaian)

📐 Rumus

KalimatRumus
➕ PositifS + would + V1
➖ NegatifS + would + not + V1
❓ TanyaWould + S + V1 ?
ℹ️ Tenses ini sering dipakai dalam kalimat pengandaian (Conditional Sentence Type 2): If I had money, I would buy a car.

💬 Contoh Kalimat

➕ She said she would come to the party.
➖ He said he would not help us.
➕ If I had time, I would learn guitar.
